A Multi-Billion Dollar Market Driven by Urgent Need

The demand for affordable, scalable housing solutions is critical. The global modular construction market is valued at over $104 billion and is projected to exceed $140 billion by 2029.

Africa

Africa's urban population is expected to nearly triple by 2050, creating unprecedented demand for housing.

South America

A severe housing deficit that exceeds 5.8 million units in Brazil alone.

Southeast Asia

Urgent need for affordable housing to accommodate rapid urbanization, with a backlog of over 12.7 million units in Indonesia alone.

CT-Global.

Our mission is to address the global housing crisis by deploying technologically advanced, affordable, and sustainable communities in rural and underserved areas.
