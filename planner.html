Supplier & Logistics Research List

Actionable to-do list for gathering specific data needed to refine the project budget and plan.

B. Modular Units

  • **Task:** Identify 5-10 top-rated manufacturers on Alibaba, Made-in-China, etc.
  • **Task:** Get formal quotes for the "$1,900" and "$6,890" unit types.
  • **Data:** Price per unit (at 10, 50, 100 units).
  • **Data:** Customization options and costs.
  • **Data:** Production lead time.
  • **Data:** Flat-pack shipping specs (how many units per 40ft container?).

B. Logistics & Tariffs

  • **Task:** Research 3-5 freight forwarders (e.g., Basenton).
  • **Data:** Cost for 40ft container: China -> Mombasa (Kenya).
  • **Data:** Cost for 40ft container: China -> Lagos (Nigeria).
  • **Data:** Cost for 40ft container: China -> Durrës (Albania).
  • **Task:** Research import duties for "prefabricated buildings" in all 3 target countries.
  • **Task:** Identify local heavy-haul trucking companies near target ports.
  • **Data:** Get quote for 100km inland heavy-haul.

C. 4K Surveillance System

  • **Task:** Research bulk suppliers for 4K PoE cameras and NVRs.
  • **Suppliers:** Avigilon, Axis, Dahua, Hikvision.
  • **Data:** Price per 8-camera 4K NVR kit.
  • **Data:** Price for 1000m spool of outdoor CAT6 cable.
  • **Data:** Shipping costs and import duties for electronics in target regions.

C. Community Wi-Fi

  • **Task:** Research commercial outdoor mesh systems.
  • **Suppliers:** Calix, Eero, TP-Link (Deco), Netgear (Orbi).
  • **Data:** Price for 1x outdoor "hub" unit.
  • **Data:** Price for 10x 2-pack in-home "spoke" units.
  • **Data:** ISP/Satellite internet setup and monthly costs in rural Kenya/Nigeria/Albania.

C. Smart Utilities

  • **Task:** Research off-grid solar microgrid providers.
  • **Suppliers:** Tesla (Powerwall), or alternatives.
  • **Data:** Get quote for a system to power 10 homes (est. 30-50kW solar + 100kWh battery).
  • **Task:** Research smart water & electric meters.
  • **Suppliers:** Bluebot, Neptune, etc.
  • **Data:** Price per smart water meter (x10).
  • **Data:** Price per smart electric meter (x10).

D. Legal & Permits

  • **Task:** Identify 3 local architectural/engineering firms in each target region.
  • **Task:** Research official government sites for:
    • Building Permit ("Alvará") process (Brazil).
    • NCA registration process (Kenya).
    • Building Permit (PD 1096) process (Philippines - for future).
  • **Data:** Estimated cost for an Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) in Kenya.
