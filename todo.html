Project To-Do List

Actionable tasks organized by category to refine the project budget and plan

B. Modular Units - Supplier Research

Task: Identify 5-10 top-rated manufacturers on Alibaba, Made-in-China, etc.
Task: Get formal quotes for the "$1,900" and "$6,890" unit types
  • Data Needed: Price per unit (at 10, 50, 100 units)
  • Data Needed: Customization options and costs
  • Data Needed: Production lead time
  • Data Needed: Flat-pack shipping specs (how many units per 40ft container?)

B. Logistics & Tariffs - Freight Research

Task: Research 3-5 freight forwarders (e.g., Basenton)
  • Data Needed: Cost for 40ft container: China → Mombasa (Kenya)
  • Data Needed: Cost for 40ft container: China → Lagos (Nigeria)
  • Data Needed: Cost for 40ft container: China → Durrës (Albania)
Task: Research import duties for "prefabricated buildings" in all 3 target countries
Task: Identify local heavy-haul trucking companies near target ports
  • Data Needed: Get quote for 100km inland heavy-haul

C. 4K Surveillance System - Security Equipment

Task: Research bulk suppliers for 4K PoE cameras and NVRs
  • Suppliers: Avigilon, Axis, Dahua, Hikvision
  • Data Needed: Price per 8-camera 4K NVR kit
  • Data Needed: Price for 1000m spool of outdoor CAT6 cable
  • Data Needed: Shipping costs and import duties for electronics in target regions

C. Community Wi-Fi - Network Infrastructure

Task: Research commercial outdoor mesh systems
  • Suppliers: Calix, Eero, TP-Link (Deco), Netgear (Orbi)
  • Data Needed: Price for 1x outdoor "hub" unit
  • Data Needed: Price for 10x 2-pack in-home "spoke" units
  • Data Needed: ISP/Satellite internet setup and monthly costs in rural Kenya/Nigeria/Albania

C. Smart Utilities - Energy & Water Management

Task: Research off-grid solar microgrid providers
  • Suppliers: Tesla (Powerwall), or alternatives
  • Data Needed: Get quote for a system to power 10 homes (est. 30-50kW solar + 100kWh battery)
Task: Research smart water & electric meters
  • Suppliers: Bluebot, Neptune, etc.
  • Data Needed: Price per smart water meter (x10)
  • Data Needed: Price per smart electric meter (x10)

D. Legal & Permits - Regulatory Compliance

Task: Identify 3 local architectural/engineering firms in each target region
Task: Research official government sites for:
  • Building Permit ("Alvará") process (Brazil)
  • NCA registration process (Kenya)
  • Building Permit (PD 1096) process (Philippines - for future)
Data Needed: Estimated cost for an Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) in Kenya
