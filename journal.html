October 15, 2025 Product Sourcing

Product Options Identified

Identified two primary modular unit options for pilot projects:

  • Expandable Foldable Unit (~$1,900 USD): Basic rapid-deployment module suitable for utility, storage, or temporary housing. Ideal for auxiliary structures in larger developments.
  • 40ft Expandable Home (~$6,890 USD): Complete 2-bedroom home that expands on-site. Modern, spacious living environment suitable for permanent residential communities.

Both units offer significant cost savings compared to traditional construction while maintaining quality and durability standards.

How to Update This Journal

To add new entries to this journal, follow these steps:

  1. Open the journal.html file in a text editor
  2. Copy one of the existing journal entry blocks (the entire <div class="journal-entry">...</div> section)
  3. Paste it at the top of the journal entries section (right after the page header)
  4. Update the date, category, title, and content with your new information
  5. Save the file and push to GitHub to publish

Pro Tip: Keep entries concise and focused on key milestones, decisions, or insights. Use bullet points for clarity.
