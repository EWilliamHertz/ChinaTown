Partner With Us to Build the Future

We are actively seeking strategic partners to accelerate our mission. We have opportunities for manufacturers, investors, and public sector organizations.

For Manufacturers

We are seeking forward-thinking manufacturing partners to create a reliable supply chain for high-quality, cost-effective modular homes. Partner with us to provide the physical solution for a global challenge.

For Investors

Our venture offers a unique opportunity to generate both significant financial returns and measurable social impact. We have viable funding models for every stage, from a 10-unit pilot project to large-scale deployment, leveraging impact investing, VC, and project finance.

For Governments & NGOs

Our 'Smart Town as a Service' model is the ideal solution for large-scale affordable housing initiatives. We deliver complete, managed, and secure communities, not just housing units—addressing critical concerns like security and resource management from day one.

CT-Global.

Our mission is to address the global housing crisis by deploying technologically advanced, affordable, and sustainable communities in rural and underserved areas.
