## Key Information from Documents

### Product Options
1. **Expandable Foldable Unit**: ~$1,900 USD per unit
   - Image: unit_1900_option.webp
   - Basic, rapid-deployment module for utility, storage, or temporary housing

2. **40ft Expandable Home**: ~$6,890 USD per unit
   - Images: unit_6890_option_floorplan.webp, unit_6890_option_interior.webp
   - Complete 2-bedroom home, expands on-site
   - Modern, spacious living environment

### Business Model (from MessageToProducers.doc)
- Partnership opportunity for modular housing
- "CT-China Town" concept - scalable, "Lego-like" building strategy
- Turnkey solution with Smart Town features:
  - 4K camera surveillance system
  - Community-wide smart grids
  - Wi-Fi access points
  - Sustainable utilities

### Market Analysis (from ModularHousingBusinessPlanResearch.doc)
- Global modular construction market: $104B (2024) → $140B+ (2029)
- Target regions: Africa, South America, Southeast Asia
- Key competitors: Karmod (Turkey), Kwikspace (South Africa)
- Unique differentiator: "Smart Town as a Service" model
